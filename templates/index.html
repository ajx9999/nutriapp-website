NUTRIAPP

Mission and Vision

◎

Mission

To help users make healthier food choices by providing simple, personalized nutrition guidance, dietary goal tracking, and useful meal recommendations.

◉

Vision

To become an accessible and trusted mobile platform that helps people build healthier eating habits and improve their long-term well-being.

PRODUCT OBJECTIVES

Product Objectives

The product aims to provide users with tools to manage their nutrition and dietary goals effectively. It should allow users to:

✓

Create and manage a personal health profile.

✓

Calculate recommended daily calorie intake based on user information.

✓

Choose dietary preferences or diet types.

✓

Set nutritional and caloric goals for better diet.

✓

Track weight, calories, nutrition and dietary progress.

✓

Receive suitable meal or recipe recommendations.

✓

View progress toward dietary goals.

✓

Adjust goals as their needs change.
